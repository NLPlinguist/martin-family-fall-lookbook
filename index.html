Styling Tips

A few simple rules to make the family photos feel cohesive:

  • Anchor the group with one dominant color (e.g. Tan or Warm Cream) and use one accent color (e.g. Midnight Merlot or Vintage Rust) so photos have a shared thread.
  • Match twins in complementary tones rather than identical outfits — same palette, different pieces.
  • Keep textures warm and cozy: knits, cardigans, wool coats and cashmere reads soft on camera.
  • Let babies and toddlers lean into the softest neutrals (Warm Cream, Tan) — they photograph best in muted tones.
  • Save the metallics (Antique Gold, Champagne, Shimmering Silver) for accessories — scarves, hats, buttons — so they shimmer without overpowering.
